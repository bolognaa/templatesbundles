# Templates Bundles Privacy Policy

**App:** BESTIE Pin Scheduler by Templates Bundles
**Owner:** Templates Bundles (Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/TemplatesBundles)
**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

BESTIE Pin Scheduler is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of the Templates Bundles Pinterest business account.

- It uses the Pinterest API only to create, schedule and read Pins and boards on the owner's own account, and to read that account's analytics.
- The OAuth access token is stored privately on the owner's server and is never shared.
- It does not access, collect or store data from any other Pinterest user.
- No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Contact: via the Templates Bundles Etsy shop, https://www.etsy.com/shop/TemplatesBundles
